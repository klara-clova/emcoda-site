How this sample was made accessible

These are the things Emcoda builds into every website, and you can check them on this page:

  • Every picture has a written description for screen readers.
  • Text and background colours have strong contrast.
  • You can use the whole page with a keyboard: press Tab and look for the outline.
  • A "Skip to main content" link appears when you press Tab on arrival.
  • Headings are in order, and the table has proper column and row headings.
  • Text can be enlarged, and the page works on a phone.
  • Animation is off for visitors who ask for reduced motion.
